Belcanto Restaurant — Project Breakdown

  1. Concept & Experience Design

    From a restaurant space to an interactive digital experience.

    Designed the concept around exploration, navigation, interaction, atmosphere, and accessibility through the web.

  2. 3D Environment & Modeling

    The entire restaurant was built from scratch.

    Architecture, interiors, furniture, decorative elements, and exterior spaces were modeled and prepared in Blender for real-time production.

  3. Unreal Engine 5

    Turning the 3D model into a living environment.

    Developed the restaurant in Unreal Engine 5 with real-time lighting, materials, reflections, cameras, environments, and scene composition.

  4. Interactive Experience

    Don't just look around — explore it your way.

    Implemented first-person, third-person, and drone navigation alongside interactive elements, environmental changes, camera controls, and Blueprint systems.

  5. CGI Animation

    Price on Request

    Professional CGI animations that transform concepts into engaging visual stories for advertisements, presentations, and promotional campaigns.

  6. Website & Digital Interface

    The website becomes the entrance to the experience.

    Created a custom web interface connecting the restaurant presentation, visual content, information, and interactive Unreal Engine experience.

  7. Pixel Streaming

    From Unreal Engine to any browser.

    Connected the real-time experience through Pixel Streaming, allowing visitors to explore the environment without downloading or installing the application.

  8. Final Integration

    One project. Multiple technologies. One experience.

    Combined Blender, Unreal Engine 5, Blueprint, web development, and Pixel Streaming into a complete digital hospitality experience built independently over five months.

Bugatti Divo — Project Breakdown

  1. Concept & Customer Experience

    What if the customer's perfect configuration isn't in the showroom?

    Designed as an interactive automotive experience where customers can explore and personalize a vehicle in real time instead of relying only on available physical configurations, images, or descriptions.

  2. Vehicle & Real-Time Preparation

    Transforming a high-detail supercar into an interactive digital product.

    The Bugatti Divo was prepared for real-time presentation, with vehicle components, materials, glass, lighting, interior elements, doors, hood, and wheels organized for independent control and interaction.

  3. Unreal Engine 5

    From a 3D vehicle to a premium real-time experience.

    Built in Unreal Engine 5 with real-time automotive materials, lighting, reflections, post-processing, environment design, camera systems, animation, and visual effects focused on presenting the vehicle at a luxury standard.

  4. Real-Time Configurator

    Don't just view the car — build the configuration you want.

    Developed an interactive customization system allowing users to instantly explore different body colors, wheel finishes, and interior combinations, turning vehicle personalization into part of the experience.

  5. Interactive Vehicle Experience

    The vehicle responds to the customer.

    Doors and hood can be opened and closed, vehicle lighting can be controlled, and activation effects combine visuals and sound to create a more immersive and engaging product presentation.

  6. UI, Cameras & Blueprint Systems

    Every interaction guides the customer's attention.

    Custom UI and Blueprint systems connect vehicle configuration, interactions, animations, and smooth camera transitions—including dedicated exterior views and immersive transitions into the interior during customization.

  7. Multi-Showroom Experience

    One vehicle. Different environments. Different experiences.

    Created multiple luxury showroom environments with distinct architecture, lighting, atmosphere, and supporting vehicles, allowing the Divo to be presented across different visual experiences without changing the core configurator.

  8. Final Automotive Experience

    More than a visualization — a concept for the digital showroom.

    Combined Unreal Engine 5, Blueprint development, automotive visualization, customization, UI/UX, animation, sound, cinematics, and real-time interaction into one complete experience exploring how digital technology could support vehicle presentation, personalization, customer engagement, and the automotive sales journey.
